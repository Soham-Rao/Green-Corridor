# Green Corridor System

An intelligent traffic management system that dynamically converts red signals to green, enabling faster and uninterrupted movement of emergency vehicles such as ambulances, fire trucks, and police units.

---

## Overview

Traffic congestion is one of the major causes of delayed emergency response times in urban environments. The **Green Corridor System** addresses this problem by creating a real-time, automated corridor for emergency vehicles by prioritizing their movement through traffic signals.

This system detects incoming emergency vehicles and optimizes traffic signals along their route to ensure minimal delays.

---

## Objectives

* Reduce emergency vehicle response time
* Automate traffic signal prioritization
* Improve urban traffic efficiency
* Minimize human intervention in critical situations

## Key Features
